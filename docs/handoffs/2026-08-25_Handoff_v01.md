## Current state

Works. The MVP answers all six success criteria in the project brief.

Findings worth carrying forward:

1. **The lowest federal rate for 2026 is 14%, not 15%.** The 2025 cut applies
   in full, and that rate also converts credits, so it moves every Canadian
   figure.
2. **CRA's public tax brackets page shows 5.6% for BC's first bracket.** The
   province and CRA's own T4127 payroll formulas both give 5.06%. Caught by
   verifying the rate tables against the bracket constants CRA publishes.
   Worth reporting to CRA.
3. **The FX versus PPP toggle barely moves the ratio.** The same rate converts
   the gross in and the net back out, so it largely cancels and survives only
   through tax non-linearity. Stated on the methodology page rather than left
   for a reader to discover and mistrust.
4. **The Austrian special payment regime is worth 1,391 to 13,417 euro a year**
   across the modelled range, confirming the brief's claim that omitting it
   materially understated Austrian compensation.
5. **Austrian special payments carry a lower social insurance rate** (17.07%
   against 18.07%) because the chamber and housing levies are not charged on
   them.
6. **Vienna raised its housing levy to 1.5% from 2026**, so a Vienna employee
   pays 18.32% rather than 18.07%. The model uses the national rate and
   documents the difference.

Known limitations, all documented in the app:

- British Columbia only. Quebec is absent rather than approximated.
- No employer-side costs, no housing adjustment, no value attributed to what
  Austrian social insurance buys.
- PPP reference year is 2025 while the tax year is 2026.

## Tooling notes

- **canada.ca blocks curl and wget** on their TLS fingerprint, and resolves to
  IPv6 first. `tools/source.sh` uses a client that replays a browser
  fingerprint. This recurs at every annual refresh.
- **`vite preview` cannot be used for screenshots.** It returns 404 for the
  module script when the request carries CORS headers, which browsers send for
  a `crossorigin` module, so the app never boots. `tools/screenshot.sh` uses a
  plain static server, which is how GitHub Pages behaves.
